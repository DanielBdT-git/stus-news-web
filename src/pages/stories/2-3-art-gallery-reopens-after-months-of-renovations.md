You may have noticed several posters populating our community boards advertising various events not just for artists but for anyone interested in participating in the creative community. On the 15th of October, Grande Prairie’s very own art gallery had its Grand Reopening to wide admiration and success from many local visitors. Three brand new exhibitions opened that day, each of which great in its own right. Several members of NWP faculty were involved with curating these exhibitions as well, with our very own Mohsen Ahi Andy and Laura Motta curating and exhibiting in the gallery. Three initial exhibitions each opened to a fantastic opening night and a promising outlook for the future of the gallery.
