X-country provincials were held this weekend in Red Deer, where despite the cold temperatures, the wind was much calmer than a previous race at the same location. The fastest man for NWP was Sharif Akali and the fastest woman for NWP was Abby Breuls de Tiecken. A banquet was held afterwards where awards were handed out to individuals and teams.
