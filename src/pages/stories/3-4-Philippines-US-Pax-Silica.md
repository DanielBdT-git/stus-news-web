There has been collective outrage amongst Filipinos against a proposed Pax Silica hub. New Clark City was the location for the Philippine hub, which is 1,620 hectares. The Philippines has been working on assembling packaging of chips, which has been an important part of the economy for a long time. However, this will change as The Philippines is adding the beginning processes to have a chance to be more competitive. Now in regards to the opposition, there have been many groups opposing this plan through social media. All of these problems start at the government, having provided the US a strategic location unconditionally for 2 years, essentially being rent-free. Furthermore, there has been a demand for congress to answer questions such as the true range of the jobs, how many Filipinos will work in senior roles and will they be treated well? Next would be the land, Pax Silica is planned on public land which is supposed to be used for industrial development. But this will also affect nearby towns’ access to the land, and how water will be redistributed to the residents, taking surface water which is detrimental for dry seasons by effects of climate change. What will happen to the already established livelihoods of the Indigenous Aeta? And finally, there have been skeptics about the true control the Philippines has. Though the hub has not been initiated, many questions are being asked by the Filipinos, they want transparency, structured evidence and data.
