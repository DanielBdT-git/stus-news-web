As of November 14th, Ron Thomson became a permanent part of Northwestern Polytechnic via his induction into the NWP Wolves Hall of Fame. Over the course of the past 30 years, his legacy came through many achievements, as well as his lasting impression on the athletes he coached. His career is full of success, including: over 500 competitive victories, five Volleyball Coach of the Year awards, two Canadian Coach of Excellence awards, a Volleyball Alberta Hall of Fame induction, and leading teams to 17 medals. As well as excelling in leadership, President and CEO of NWP, Dr. Vanessa Sheane, also credits Ron’s, “commitment to mentorship and student success”.
