The proposed Bill 38 aims for two main effects; establish caps for raising tuitions and enforce institutional neutrality on political or controversial topics. When put under further scrutiny it seems that neither statement is actually true. 
 Under Bill 38 tuition costs would still increase at a rate of 2%, still out of reach for the average student without taking out loans.Alberta Regulations states the average cap for tuition raises now is already 2%. 
 “It’s a huge waste of time. Institutions all have their standards by which they operate under ... This government is trying to bring forward this culture war garbage,” Alberta NDP shadow minister David Eggen told CTV.
